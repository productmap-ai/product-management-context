# Retrospective template

**Template:** Delivery / Team improvement

## Purpose

Run a structured retro so the team reflects on what went well, what didn’t, and what to change next iteration.

## Suggested structure

1. **Set the stage** — reminder of retro goals and safety (blameless)
2. **Gather data** — what happened (facts, not judgment)
3. **Generate insights** — why did things happen; patterns
4. **Decide what to do** — 1–3 concrete actions with owners
5. **Close** — appreciation or one-word wrap; schedule follow-up

## Formats

- Start / stop / continue
- Mad / sad / glad
- 4 L’s (Loved, Learned, Lacked, Longed for)
- Timeline or sailboat
