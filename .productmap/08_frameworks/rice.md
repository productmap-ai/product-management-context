# RICE

**Framework:** Prioritization

## Summary

RICE scores ideas using **R**each, **I**mpact, **C**onfidence, and **E**ffort to compare and rank initiatives.

## Formula

**RICE score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort**

- **Reach** — how many users/outcomes per period (e.g. per quarter)
- **Impact** — per user/outcome impact (e.g. 3 = massive, 2 = high, 1 = medium, 0.5 = low, 0.25 = minimal)
- **Confidence** — how sure you are in the estimates (e.g. 100%, 80%, 50%)
- **Effort** — person-months (or similar) to deliver

## When to use

- Prioritizing a backlog of features or initiatives
- When you have rough estimates and want a comparable score
- As input to roadmap and planning discussions
