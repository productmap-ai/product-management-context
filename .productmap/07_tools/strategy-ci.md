## Tools

### Crayon
- **Pricing:** Custom (mid-market and enterprise)
- **Best PM use cases:** Real-time competitor tracking, battlecard creation, win/loss analysis, market movement alerts
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Competitive analysis, battlecards, market landscape report

### Klue
- **Pricing:** Custom
- **Best PM use cases:** AI-powered competitive intelligence, battlecard distribution to sales, win/loss integration
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Competitive battlecards, win/loss analysis, competitor positioning map

### Similarweb
- **Pricing:** Free (limited) / Starter $125/mo / Professional $333/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** Competitor web traffic analysis, digital market share estimation, channel mix benchmarking
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Competitive traffic analysis, market share estimate

### AlphaSense
- **Pricing:** Enterprise custom pricing
- **Best PM use cases:** Financial document analysis, earnings call search, expert network transcripts for deep market research
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Market analysis, investment thesis, strategic research briefs

### Crunchbase
- **Pricing:** Free (limited) / Pro $49/mo / Enterprise custom
- **Best PM use cases:** Startup tracking, funding round monitoring, investor landscape, M&A signals
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Competitive landscape map, funding analysis, partner prospect lists

### Semrush
- **Pricing:** Pro $130/mo / Guru $250/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** SEO competitive analysis, keyword research, content gap analysis, paid search intelligence
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Keyword strategy, content brief, SEO competitor analysis
