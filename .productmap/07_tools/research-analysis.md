## Tools

### Perplexity
- **Pricing:** Free / Pro $20/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** Market research, competitor deep-dives, technology landscape scans, quick fact-checking
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Competitive analysis, market sizing, trend reports

### Dovetail AI
- **Pricing:** Starter $29/mo / Team $99/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** User interview repository, AI-assisted tagging and theme identification, research synthesis
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Synthesis report, insight clusters, persona inputs

### Gong
- **Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing (~$100–150/user/year)
- **Best PM use cases:** Sales call analysis, customer language extraction, win/loss patterns
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** ICP refinement, messaging framework, objection handling

### Elicit
- **Pricing:** Free / Plus $10/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** Academic and scientific literature review, evidence synthesis for data-informed decisions
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Research summaries, evidence tables

### Consensus
- **Pricing:** Free / Premium $10/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** Scientific research queries with cited results, background research for product decisions
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Evidence-backed research briefs

### ChatPRD
- **Pricing:** Free / Pro $12/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** AI-assisted PRD creation, user story generation, product specification drafting
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** PRD, user stories, acceptance criteria

### NotebookLM (Google)
- **Pricing:** Free (with Google account)
- **Best PM use cases:** Document-grounded Q&A, multi-source synthesis, podcast-style audio summaries of documents
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Research synthesis, document summaries, briefing notes
