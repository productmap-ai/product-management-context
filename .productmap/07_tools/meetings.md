# Meetings — AI Tools

> Draft — Tools / Communication & Meetings

Communication and meeting tools reduce the overhead of synchronous and async collaboration by automatically transcribing, summarising, and extracting action items from conversations — freeing PMs to focus on the discussion rather than note-taking.

## Tools

### Otter.ai
- **Pricing:** Free (300 min/mo) / Pro $16.99/mo / Business $30/mo per user
- **Best PM use cases:** Meeting transcription, action item extraction, speaker identification, searchable meeting archive
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Meeting notes, action items, interview transcripts

### Fireflies.ai
- **Pricing:** Free / Pro $18/mo / Business $29/mo per user
- **Best PM use cases:** Automatic meeting recording and transcription, CRM integration, conversation analytics
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Meeting summaries, action logs, conversation intelligence reports

### Fathom
- **Pricing:** Free / Premium $19/mo / Team Edition $29/mo per user
- **Best PM use cases:** AI-generated meeting highlights and summaries, zero-effort note-taking, Zoom/Meet/Teams integration
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Meeting summaries, highlight clips, action items

### tl;dv
- **Pricing:** Free / Pro $25/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** Research call recording with timestamped highlights, user interview clip sharing
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Interview highlights, research clips, meeting summaries

### Loom AI
- **Pricing:** Starter free / Business $15/user/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** Async video updates with AI-generated summaries and transcripts, product walkthroughs
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Async stakeholder updates, product demos, onboarding videos

### Grain
- **Pricing:** Free / Starter $19/mo / Business $39/mo per user
- **Best PM use cases:** Meeting highlight reels, customer research clip libraries, AI-powered note templates
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Research highlight reels, customer evidence clips

### Gong
- **Pricing:** Enterprise custom (~$1,200–1,600/user/year)
- **Best PM use cases:** Sales and customer call analysis, conversation intelligence, deal risk assessment
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Win/loss analysis, customer insight report, coaching recommendations
