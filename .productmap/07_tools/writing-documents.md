## Tools

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- **Pricing:** Free / Plus $20/mo / Team $25/mo per user
- **Best PM use cases:** PRD drafts, user story writing, stakeholder email drafting, brainstorming, meeting prep
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** PRD, one-pager, competitive analysis, OKRs, interview guides

### Claude (Anthropic)
- **Pricing:** Free / Pro $20/mo / Team $25/mo per user
- **Best PM use cases:** Long-document synthesis, nuanced analysis, detailed PRDs, instruction-following tasks
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** PRD, research synthesis, positioning doc, decision brief

### Gemini (Google)
- **Pricing:** Free / Advanced $20/mo (included in Google One AI Premium)
- **Best PM use cases:** Google Docs/Sheets/Slides integration, real-time search-grounded answers
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Board decks, forecasts, roadmap docs, meeting summaries

### Microsoft Copilot
- **Pricing:** Included with Microsoft 365 Business plans / $30/mo add-on
- **Best PM use cases:** Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Outlook, Teams integration
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Board decks, status reports, financial models, email drafts

### Notion AI
- **Pricing:** $10/mo add-on per workspace member
- **Best PM use cases:** In-Notion document drafting, meeting note summarisation, action item extraction
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Decision log, sprint notes, stakeholder updates, knowledge base

### Jasper
- **Pricing:** Creator $49/mo / Pro $69/mo
- **Best PM use cases:** Marketing copy, product descriptions, launch announcements, SEO content
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** GTM messaging, landing page copy, email sequences

### Grammarly AI
- **Pricing:** Free / Premium $12/mo / Business $15/mo per user
- **Best PM use cases:** Real-time writing improvement, tone adjustment, professional email polishing
- **Key artefacts it helps create:** Any written communication — polishing rather than drafting
